## Design Context

### Users
Cybersecurity professionals (security engineers, SOC analysts, pentesters, GRC practitioners) who want a professional portfolio site without web development experience. They clone this template, fill in their info, and deploy. The demo needs to look polished enough that they think "I want my portfolio to look like this."

### Brand Personality
**Credible, technical, understated.** Not flashy, not corporate — the quiet confidence of someone who knows what they're doing. Think: a senior engineer's personal site that earns trust through restraint, not through visual noise.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme**: GitHub-inspired dark mode as default. `#0d1117` background, `#58a6ff` blue accent, `#161b22` card surfaces.
- **Typography**: Alata (headings, nav, UI) + Lora (body text, editorial feel). Tight heading letter-spacing, generous body line-height.
- **Cards**: Minimal borders (`rgba(255,255,255,0.06)`), subtle hover lift, no glassmorphism or glow effects.
- **Spacing**: Compact sections (3rem padding, not 5rem), let content breathe without wasteful whitespace.
- **References**: ethantroy.dev (et-fish/Blowfish, github color scheme), coderon-hugo (visual density, bold cards)
- **Anti-references**: Generic Bootstrap templates, cyan-on-dark "AI slop" palette, glassmorphism, gradient text, neon glow borders, identical card grids with icons above every heading

### Design Principles
1. **Earn trust through restraint** — every visual element should feel deliberate, not decorative
2. **Content over chrome** — the portfolio content (projects, experience, certs) is the star, not the CSS effects
3. **Dark mode first** — images and palette are designed for dark backgrounds; light mode is the secondary experience
4. **Editorial typography** — serif body text (Lora) gives weight to the words; geometric sans headings (Alata) provide structure
5. **No AI fingerprints** — avoid cyan accents, purple gradients, glassmorphism, bounce animations, and everything else that screams "generated"
